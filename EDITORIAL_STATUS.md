# EmpowerED Strategies: Editorial Redesign

Updated: 13 November 2026

## Visual direction

The website follows the approved institutional positioning in the 35-page master brief and uses four user-supplied editorial design references. The references inform the composition and art direction only; they are not republished as EmpowerED project photography.

### Colour system

- Core plum: `#50214F`
- Core red: `#E73743`
- Near-black plum: `#160E1B`
- Warm editorial paper: `#F8F4EE`
- White paper: `#FFFDF8`
- Dusty mauve: `#EBDFE5`

### Typography

Creative display families: Unbounded, Syne, Bricolage Grotesque and Bodoni Moda. Space Mono serves micro labels, page folios and indexes. DM Sans is used for readable body copy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; no proprietary or redistributed font files are included.

### Page-by-page art direction

1. **Home:** Dark cinematic magazine cover, layered learning photography, red light effects, oversized background wordmark and strong editorial display type.
2. **About Us:** Burgundy dossier with portrait, expressive serif title and institutional profile.
3. **What We Do:** Dark typographic contents index with six long-form service divisions.
4. **Solutions:** High-contrast cream/red editorial product catalogue, asymmetrical image-led stories.
5. **Learning Academy:** Dusty mauve course catalogue with interactive programme outlines and practical-learning philosophy.
6. **Research & Insights:** Paper-like research journal with publication series and a Future of Learning Forum introduction.
7. **Our Impact:** Deep plum verification-led statement. No fabricated metrics, projects or partner endorsements.
8. **Partnerships:** Dark editorial feature with collaboration photography and partnership opportunities.
9. **Contact:** Calm editorial consultation form. Form currently prepares an email rather than silently claiming to submit to a server.
10. **Four product pages:** Consistent institutional product dossiers with differentiated content.

## Brand asset

In the connected Think Tank repository, the EmpowerED partner asset is at:

`public/images/partners/empowered-strategies.webp`

The GitHub connector exposes that file path but does not decode the binary WebP asset. The current website therefore references the corresponding existing Think Tank custom domain asset path:

`https://www.thinktankcircle.org/images/partners/empowered-strategies.webp`

If the image responds successfully in the visitor's browser, it replaces the interim typography wordmark. Otherwise, the text wordmark remains. For a fully independent site, the approved asset should ultimately be copied into the EmpowerED repository and served locally; this remote URL has not been independently verified from this environment.

## Implemented frontend

The public-facing pages, service and programme text, navigation, Academy programme outlines, contact email composition, favicon, canonical URLs and sitemap exist in the repository.

## Backend and verification still required

The master brief requests more than a visual frontend. Items **not represented as finished** include:

- Server-side consultation and enquiry submissions with confirmation emails and departmental routing.
- Academy applications, enrolment administration, cohort tracking, certificates, and eventually an LMS.
- Research & Insights publishing CMS and asset uploads.
- Verified case study administration and approved project evidence.
- Newsletter subscription storage, consent and delivery integration.
- Conversion analytics and dashboards.
- Verified staff biographies, business phone/address, publication dates and externally approved materials.
- Independently tested live image and logo delivery.
- Full mobile and cross-browser visual quality assurance after the latest Vercel deployment.

The goal is to preserve an accurate, credible website while the operational integrations are added separately.
